Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 8, 2025

Welcome to Compete NG! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application. By accessing or using Compete NG, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect personal information you provide to us, including your name, email address, and profile information. This information is necessary for account creation, personalized goal setting, and user engagement.

Fitness and Goal Data

Compete NG collects data related to your fitness activities and goals, including recorded exercises, achievements, and collaborative commitments with friends or family members. This data helps improve the app's functionality and personalize goal tracking features.

We also collect data on whether a user chooses to appear on the community leaderboard or be included in social media posts. You can control this visibility through the app settings.

Device Information

We automatically collect information about the device you use to access Compete NG, including the device model, operating system, and unique device identifiers. This helps us optimize the app for your device.

Chat Data

If you use the team chat feature, we collect and store the messages you send and receive to facilitate communication between team members. This data is only visible to users who are part of the same team.

How We Use Your Information

Personalized Fitness Assistance

Your personal information and fitness data are used to provide collaborative goal-setting features and relevant fitness content, enabling Compete NG to assist you in establishing and committing to personalized fitness routines.

Collaborative Features

Compete NG uses your fitness and goal data to facilitate collaborative efforts with friends or family members, including shared commitments, penalty decisions, and mutual support mechanisms. Your visibility settings are respected at all times.

Team Communication

Chat data is used to enable team members to communicate effectively within the app, improving collaboration and accountability among users pursuing shared fitness goals.

App Improvement

We analyze aggregated and anonymized user data to improve Compete NG's features, functionality, and overall user experience.

Information Sharing and Disclosure

Information Sharing

Compete NG enables users to share certain fitness and goal data with friends or family members for collaborative purposes. If you choose to appear on the community leaderboard or be included in social media posts, your information may be shared publicly within the app or on our social media platforms. You can opt out at any time through the app settings.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your personal information if required by law or in response to valid legal processes, such as a court order or government request.

Chat Data Sharing

Messages sent through the team chat feature are visible only to users who are part of the same team. Compete NG does not share chat data with any third parties except as required by law.

Data Security

Compete NG is committed to ensuring the security of your personal information. We employ industry-standard measures to protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of your data.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information, fitness and goal data, and chat data for as long as your account remains active, in order to provide the collaborative features Compete NG is built around.

If you delete your account, we permanently delete your personal information, fitness and goal data, and chat data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes. You can request account and data deletion at any time — see our Data Deletion page for instructions.

Aggregated or anonymized data that no longer identifies you may be retained beyond this period for app improvement purposes.

Consent and Changes to This Policy

By using Compete NG, you consent to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our data practices. We will notify users about important changes through the app or via email.
